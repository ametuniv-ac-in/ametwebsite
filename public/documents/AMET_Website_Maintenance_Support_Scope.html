Website Maintenance & Support Scope

For AMET University Website — content updates, technical support, enhancements, and ongoing care.

Prepared for Client ReviewMonthly Support Scope2026

This document outlines the proposed scope for maintaining and supporting the AMET University website. The engagement covers routine website updates, new content publishing, creation of new pages or sections, technical monitoring, and timely support for website-related requirements.

1. Scope of Website Maintenance

Content Updates

  • Update text, images, banners, announcements, notices, circulars, and contact details.
  • Upload and link PDFs such as exam notices, timetables, results, forms, prospectus, and policy documents.
  • Update department, admission, placement, governance, and student-corner content.
  • Correct spelling, formatting, layout alignment, and broken content issues.

Existing Site Edits

  • Edit existing pages, menus, cards, tables, accordions, sliders, and footer/header information.
  • Replace outdated content with approved new content.
  • Maintain consistent styling, brand colors, responsive layout, and link behavior.
  • Improve usability and presentation of existing sections where required.

New Page / Section Creation

  • Create new pages for departments, events, announcements, statutory disclosures, courses, or initiatives.
  • Create landing sections, galleries, document lists, profile pages, and structured content blocks.
  • Add navigation links and ensure pages work across desktop and mobile devices.
  • Follow the approved website design system and content hierarchy.

Document & Media Management

  • Organize files in website document folders with meaningful names.
  • Ensure PDF links open correctly and downloadable documents work as expected.
  • Compress and optimize images where needed for faster loading.
  • Replace obsolete files and maintain clean document references.

2. Technical Support & Monitoring

  • Check and fix broken links, missing pages, incorrect redirects, and display issues.
  • Resolve layout problems on desktop, tablet, and mobile views.
  • Monitor website performance and recommend improvements for speed and user experience.
  • Review dependency or front-end issues and apply safe updates when needed.
  • Support publishing and deployment of approved website changes.

3. SEO, Analytics & Compliance Support

  • Update page titles, meta descriptions, headings, and structured content where applicable.
  • Maintain clean internal linking and search-friendly page structure.
  • Support analytics and tracking updates when requested.
  • Ensure statutory and mandatory disclosure pages remain organized and accessible.

4. Request Handling Process

StepProcess
1Client shares approved content, documents, images, and instructions by email or agreed channel.
2Support team reviews the request, confirms scope, and estimates effort or timeline if required.
3Changes are implemented on the website and checked for layout, links, and responsiveness.
4Client reviews the update and shares feedback, if any.
5Final corrections are completed and the update is considered closed.

6. Items Included

  • Routine website content maintenance and updates.
  • Creation of new pages or sections using existing website design patterns.
  • Uploading and linking approved documents and media.
  • Basic SEO/meta updates for edited or newly created pages.
  • Responsive checks for updated pages.
  • Bug fixing related to website display, navigation, and links.

7. Items Outside Standard Scope

  • Major redesign of the entire website or brand identity.
  • Large custom software modules, portals, payment systems, or backend applications unless separately scoped.
  • Content writing, translation, photography, video production, or data entry at large scale unless agreed.
  • Third-party service charges, hosting/domain costs, paid plugins, paid APIs, or license fees.
  • Emergency work outside agreed support hours unless specifically approved.
